Our story

Built by people who show up.

Folks started with a simple frustration: finding someone reliable to fix a leaking tap or deep-clean an apartment shouldn't take three phone calls and a leap of faith. So we built a place where every professional is checked, trained and rated — and every customer knows exactly what they're paying for, before they book.

Why we exist

Home services in most cities run on word-of-mouth and luck — a neighbour's recommendation, a number scrawled on a pamphlet, a stranger who may or may not turn up. We thought homes deserved better than luck.

Folks was founded in Bengaluru with one rule: nobody gets listed unless we'd trust them in our own home. That meant building a verification process from scratch, training every professional on the specifics of their craft, and being upfront about pricing before a single tool comes out of the bag.

Today, thousands of professionals earn a steady, respected livelihood through Folks, and hundreds of thousands of households trust us with the small and large jobs that keep a home running.

What we stand for

The values behind every booking

Craft over shortcuts

Structured training in every category means the work holds up long after the professional leaves.

Fair pay and steady work for professionals; fair pricing and dependable service for customers.

Community first

We hire locally, train locally, and reinvest in the neighbourhoods that make up our cities.
